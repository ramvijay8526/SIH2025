Sikkim's Story

A land steeped in ancient traditions and breathtaking natural beauty.

Sikkim, nestled in the Himalayan heights, has a rich history dating back to the 17th century when the Namgyal dynasty ruled. The state is a melting pot of Tibetan, Nepali and Bhutia cultures, home to ancient monasteries, rhododendron forests and crystal-blue glacial lakes.

Monastery Visitor Disclaimer

Please respect the sacredness of monasteries by following these guidelines:

✅ Do’s

  • Maintain silence inside monastery premises.
  • Dress modestly and respectfully.
  • Remove shoes before entering prayer halls.
  • Seek permission before taking photos.
  • Respect ongoing prayers and rituals.

❌ Don’ts

  • Do not touch sacred objects or statues.
  • Avoid smoking, drinking, or littering.
  • Do not interrupt monks during prayers.
  • Avoid loud conversations or phone use.
  • Do not step on prayer mats or offerings.
